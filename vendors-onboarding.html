📝 2. Creating Your Vendor Account

Step-by-Step Account Setup

Follow these steps to create your vendor account and get approved to start listing your vehicles.

1

Submit Your Email to Start Registration

Begin by providing your email address to initiate the registration process:

https://carsreservation.com/vendor/register

Your account will be created automatically once you submit your email.

2

Check Your Inbox and Set Password

Check your inbox for a follow-up email containing a link to set your password.

💡 Tip: Don't forget to check your spam folder if you don't see the email within a few minutes.

3

Complete Your Profile and Accept Terms

After setting your password, complete your profile with the required details:

  • Vendor name (required) - Your business or personal name
  • Vendor email (required) - Your primary contact email
  • Phone (optional) - Contact phone number
  • PayPal email (optional) - For receiving payments via PayPal
  • VAT/SSN (optional) - Tax identification number

Before submitting, you must:

  • ☑️ Accept the Terms & Conditions for Vendors (required)
  • ☑️ Accept the Privacy Policy for Vendors (required)

Click "Become a vendor" to submit your application and wait for vendor approval. Our team will review your information within 2-3 business days.

4

Set Up Your Store Settings (Optional)

After registration, you can customize your store with additional details:

  • Store information: Store name, slug, and description
  • Contact details: Address, phone number, email, and website
  • Business information: CIF/VAT number for tax purposes
  • Store branding: Add your logo and store banner
  • Company legal notes: Terms and policies for your business

💡 Note: These store settings can be configured anytime after your account is approved.

5

Set Up Payment Information (After Approval)

Once your vendor account is approved, access your dashboard to set up payment details:

  • Account name - Your bank account holder name
  • IBAN - International bank account number
  • SWIFT/BIC - Bank identifier code for international transfers
  • PayPal email - Alternative payment method (if preferred)

Navigate to Payment info in your vendor dashboard and click "Save Payments Info" when complete.

💡 Important: Payment information must be set up before you can receive earnings from bookings.

❓ 6. Support & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using the CarsReservation vendor platform.

Getting Started

How long does vendor approval take?

Typically 2-3 business days after submitting all required documents. We may contact you if additional information is needed.

What documents do I need to become a vendor?

Business license, insurance certificate, tax registration, government-issued ID, and vehicle registration documents for each car you want to list.

Is there a fee to join as a vendor?

No joining fee. We only charge a 15% commission on successful bookings. You keep 85% of the rental fee.

Managing Listings

How many cars can I list?

No limit. You can list as many vehicles as you have proper documentation for.

Can I modify my prices anytime?

Yes, you can update pricing anytime. Changes apply to new bookings immediately. Existing confirmed bookings maintain their original price.

What if my car needs maintenance?

Block the dates in your calendar. The system will automatically prevent new bookings during blocked periods.

Bookings & Payments

When do I get paid?

Payment is processed 24 hours after pickup confirmation. Funds are transferred to your registered bank account within 2-3 business days.

What if a customer damages my car?

Document damage with photos and report it through the platform. Security deposits can be used to cover damages. Our support team helps resolve disputes.

Can customers cancel bookings?

Yes, according to the cancellation policy you set. You can choose from flexible, moderate, or strict cancellation policies.

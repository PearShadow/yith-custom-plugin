Our detailed technical SEO audit, powered by industry-standard tools like Lighthouse and Screaming Frog, provides actionable insights to improve your website's performance, visibility, and user experience. Here’s what we cover:

  • Executive Summary: A clear overview of findings with an overall estimated SEO Score (1-10) to quickly gauge your site's health.
  • Lighthouse Analysis: In-depth scores and metrics for Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO on both Desktop and Mobile.
  • Core Web Vitals: Focus on Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Total Blocking Time (TBT), Speed Index, and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
  • On-Page SEO Elements: Analysis of page titles, meta descriptions, H1 tags, and image alt text (based on Screaming Frog crawl data).
  • Crawlability & Indexation: Review of HTTP response codes, crawl depth, and canonicalization.
  • Actionable Recommendations: Clear "Must Have," "Could/Should Have," and "Fine" categorizations for prioritized improvements.
  • Data Visualizations: Easy-to-understand charts and graphs to illustrate key findings.
